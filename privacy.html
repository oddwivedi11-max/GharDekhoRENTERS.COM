Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

GharDekho Renters (“GharDekho”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains, at a general level, how information may be handled when you use our rental property discovery platform.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, account details, property information, visit requests and other information you choose to submit.

2. How information is used

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To help renters discover rental properties and communicate with owners through platform features.
  • To allow owners to create and manage property listings.
  • To process visit requests and provide support.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the platform.
  • To respond to enquiries, reports and legal or privacy requests.

3. Property and user information

Information included in property listings may be visible to other users as part of the platform's rental discovery functionality. Do not publish passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar numbers, bank credentials or other highly sensitive information in property descriptions or public fields.

4. Cookies and technical information

The website may use browser storage, cookies or similar technical mechanisms where necessary for login state, preferences, security and website functionality. Standard technical information may also be processed by hosting, security or analytics services used to operate the website.

5. Third-party services

GharDekho may rely on third-party infrastructure or service providers for functions such as authentication, database hosting, maps, email, security and website hosting. Their processing may be governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information used by the platform. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us regarding account information, privacy questions, corrections or other privacy-related requests.

8. Contact

For privacy or legal requests, contact ghardekhorenters@gmail.com.
